Find your size before you check out

Measurements are body measurements in centimetres, taken directly against skin — not the garment. Switch to inches with the toggle below any chart.

How to take your measurements

  • Chest / Bust

    Wrap the tape under your arms, across the fullest part of your chest, keeping it level around your back.

  • Waist

    Measure around your natural waistline — roughly an inch above your navel, where your torso bends side to side.

  • Hip

    Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.

  • Inseam

    Measure from the crotch seam straight down to where you'd like the trouser to end.

  • Sleeve

    With your arm slightly bent, measure from the shoulder seam, over the elbow, to the wrist bone.

Between two sizes? For tailored shirts and blazers, size down for a closer cut; for jumpers and outerwear meant to layer, size up.
